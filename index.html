About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics at the University of Maryland, College Park.

My research sits at the intersection of political economy and development economics. I study how natural resource wealth shapes who runs for and wins local office, how political dynasties entrench themselves and what they do once in power, and how gender mediates both. Most of my work is set in Indonesia and combines original administrative and electoral data with quasi-experimental designs.

Before starting the PhD, I was a Senior Research Associate at J-PAL Southeast Asia in Jakarta, where I managed nationwide randomized controlled trials on governance and social protection.
